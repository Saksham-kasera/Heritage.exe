## Brand & Style

This design system translates the timeless grace of travel literature, field chronicles, and artisanal apothecary guides into an intelligent modern utility for cultural exploration and tourist safety. The digital environment behaves like fine archival paper: quiet, measured, warm, and distinctly human. Instead of the frantic, saturated aesthetics common to commercial travel aggregators, this interface values deliberate pacing, serene white space, and authoritative typography.

### Aesthetics & Design Movement
- **Editorial Paper Aesthetic**: The foundational experience is anchored by a warm cream paper background (`#f7f1e8`) paired with stark hairline boundaries (`#e1dcd3`) and deep ink letterforms (`#1c1b1a`).
- **High Typography Scale Contrast**: A high-contrast display serif provides gravitas and historical cadence for editorial headlines, contrasted sharply with a clean, utilitarian grotesque sans for operational precision, telemetric data, and navigational controls.
- **Atmospheric Restraint**: The UI relies on crisp line structures, tonal surface transitions, and wide atmospheric gutters rather than saturated drop shadows or synthetic blurs.
- **Disciplined Color Utility**: Chromatic blues are treated as deliberate focal points reserved strictly for primary conversions and critical safety imperatives, while rich earth tones (ochre, clay, sage, forest) render radar fields, crowd telemetry, and historic classification tiers.

### Target Demographics & Sensibilities
- **Discerning Voyagers & Cultural Enthusiasts**: Travelers seeking deep historical context, architecture, culinary roots, and local narratives beyond generic top-ten tourist traps.
- **Safety-Conscious Explorers**: Users relying on uncompromised real-time weather radar, terrain safety flags, and regional advisory matrices presented with calm lucidity rather than distressful noise.
- **Emotional Response**: Grounded confidence, intellectual curiosity, tranquility, and safety.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around three distinct surface planes—warm cream paper, pristine card white, and dark ink bands—regulated by rigid chromatic discipline.

### Functional Roles & Rules
- **Primary Conversion Blue (`#2a72de`)**: Strictly reserved for high-intent conversion actions (e.g., "Confirm Itinerary", "Book Official Escort", "Activate Emergency Beacon") and active sidebar state indicators. It must never be applied to decorative borders, general icons, or secondary pills.
- **Stone Secondary (`#4a4741`)**: Anchors utilitarian components, secondary interactive buttons, secondary text links, circular icon controls, and standard outline tiers.
- **Editorial Paper Canvas (`#f7f1e8`)**: The primary default backdrop for content narratives, hero spreads, and heritage chapters.
- **Crisp Surface Elevated (`#ffffff`)**: Used for discrete modular entities, input states, inspection sheets, safety matrices, and transit dossiers.
- **Hairline (`#e1dcd3`)**: A 1px structural dividing line applied throughout editorial borders, timeline separators, and card boundaries to create structural definition without visual weight.
- **Earth Tones (Clay, Ochre, Sage, Forest)**: Dedicated solely to cultural era taxonomies, terrain elevation contours, crowd-density meters, and weather radar conditions. They must never serve as general interface buttons.

## Typography

The type system implements a strict dual-voice hierarchy:
1. **The Editorial Voice (`Newsreader`)**: A graceful serif used exclusively for editorial displays, historic narratives, literary chapter markers, and contextual quotes. It evokes classical literary journals and architectural treatises.
2. **The Rationalist Voice (`Work Sans`)**: A pristine, neutral grotesque sans used for data, navigation links, technical telemetry, buttons, tabular readouts, and body narratives.

### Typographic Principles
- **Display Scale Contrast**: High editorial drama is established by juxtaposing ultra-light display serif headlines with small, uppercase tracked sans eyebrows (`eyebrow` token with `letterSpacing: 0.08em`).
- **Body & Tabular Reading**: Default long-form reading sets `body` (18px, 1.5 line height) in neutral sans to guarantee effortless legibility when reviewing travel guides and complex transit itineraries.
- **Uppercase Eyebrows**: Every major section title should be introduced with an uppercase eyebrow tag set in `Work Sans` Bold (`eyebrow`) to prepare the reader for the thematic context.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts honor the pacing of printed broadsheets, incorporating intentional spatial pauses, broad canvas margins, and rhythmic structural divisions.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column layout with `margin: 3rem` (`48px`) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`). Editorial cards, radar views, and audio narratives follow a modular 4-column, 6-column, or full 12-column span.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` (`32px`) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (`20px`). Content reflows comfortably into 2-up card configurations.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px)**: 4-column layout with `margin: 1.25rem` (`20px`) and `gutter: 1rem` (`16px`). Multi-column editorial modules stack into a single column.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Internal Card Padding**: Standard editorial cards, itineraries, and modal sheets mandate `space-lg` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) padding on all sides. Dense flight/train schedule rows and telemetry tables drop to `space-sm` (`12px`) vertical and `space-md` (`16px`) horizontal.
- **Generous Section Pauses**: Macro content sections are separated by at least `space-xxl` (`3rem` / `48px`) up to `5rem` (`80px`), reinforcing a calm, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive, high-spread shadows in favor of a layered print-plane approach. Visual separation relies primarily on transitions between paper surfaces and crisp hairline borders.

### The Three Spatial Planes
1. **Paper Canvas Tier (Ground)**: Fills the viewport background with warm cream paper (`#f7f1e8`).
2. **Elevated Sheet Tier (Resting)**: White surfaces (`#ffffff`) bordered with a 1px solid hairline (`#e1dcd3`). This tier hosts monuments, museum guides, transit options, and safety dashboards. Elevation is communicated by tonal difference rather than heavy shadows.
3. **Stage Tier (Contrast Horizon)**: Deep ink bands (`#000000` and `#19191c`) cut horizontally across the canvas to house nocturnal safety advisories, meteorological radars, and footer retrospectives.

### Shadow Scale
- **Level 0 (Flat Outline)**: Default resting state for all standard cards, pills, and inputs. `border: 1px solid #e1dcd3; box-shadow: none;`.
- **Level 1 (Card Hover / Subtle Shift)**: Used for active itinerary cards on hover: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.04);`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Sheet / Toast)**: Reserved for tourist safety notifications, audio players, and quick-access filters: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.08);`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Scrim Overlay)**: Modal dialogs, historical inspection overlays: Backed by an ink scrim (`rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.40)`) with `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14);`.

## Shapes

The design uses a calculated duality: soft, structured card containers combined with completely rounded pills for interactive controls.

### Radius Scale
- **`rounded-none` (`0px`)**: Full-bleed hero banners, top announcement strips, atmospheric dark stage bands, and navigational dividers.
- **`rounded-xs` (`4px`)**: Architectural tags, heritage status labels, and fine micro-badges.
- **`rounded-sm` (`8px`)**: Transit table rows, dense itinerary stops, and nested list items.
- **`rounded-md` (`12px`)**: Form inputs, search fields, and selector toggles.
- **`rounded-lg` (`24px`)**: The signature content container curve. Applied to all cultural heritage cards, tour package profiles, safety alert modules, and modal containers.
- **`rounded-full` (`9999px`)**: Interactive buttons (`button-primary`, `button-secondary`), category pill tabs, stat count chips, and circular icon controls.

### Hairline Rule Integration
Every container with `rounded-lg` features a 1px continuous hairline (`#e1dcd3`) border on light canvas to retain crisp architectural integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Pill**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`). Background is `#2a72de`, foreground text is `#ffffff`, typography is `button` (`Work Sans` Semibold, 16px). Padding is 14px 28px. No drop shadow. Reserved exclusively for definitive actions: "Generate Tour Route", "Confirm Booking", "Contact Heritage Officer".
- **Secondary Stone Pill**: Background is `#4a4741`, foreground text is `#ffffff`. Used for secondary interactions such as "Audio Narrative", "Download Offline Guide".
- **Stone Outline Pill**: Transparent background, 1px border in `#4a4741`, text in `#4a4741`. Used for subtle secondary actions on cream paper.
- **Circular Icon Button**: 44×44px circular control (`rounded-full`) in `#ffffff` with a 1px `#e1dcd3` border. Accommodates next/prev sliders, audio play/pause, and map zoom.

### Chips & Pill Tabs
- **Pill Tabs**: Category selectors (`Temples`, `Forts`, `Cuisine`, `Live Radar`) feature a `#f7f1e8` or transparent background with `#4a4741` text. Active tab changes to a `#ffffff` fill with a crisp 2px border in `#cccac7` or 1px `#4a4741`.
- **Status & Safety Chips**: Compact tags with `rounded-xs` (4px). Heritage status uses `ochre` text over pale cream fill. Safety statuses: `forest` (Normal/Safe), `gold-deep` (Cautionary Weather), `error` (Restricted Zone/High Alert).

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search & Filter Inputs**: Height of 48px, `rounded-md` (12px), background `#ffffff` with a 1px `#e1dcd3` border. Placeholder text is `#838280` (`ash`).
- **Focus State**: 1.5px border transition to `#4a4741` or `#2a72de`.
- **Error State**: 1.5px border in `#e33a22` with small red explanatory caption below.

### Cards & Modular Dossiers
- **Heritage Feature Card**: Surface `#ffffff`, border 1px `#e1dcd3`, `rounded-lg` (24px), padding `space-lg` (24px). Features a top imagery element (or historical sketch) with 16px corner radius, followed by an uppercase `eyebrow` in `#838280`, a `heading-2` serif title, and succinct `body` text.
- **Radar & Telemetry Card**: Embedded directly on the cream canvas or in an ink stage band. Utilizes thin hairline divider grids to present crowd index, rainfall probability, and heat index numbers rendered in `heading-3` sans.

### Lists & Timelines
- **Itinerary Timeline**: Vertical 1px hairline line in `#e1dcd3` connecting circular stop nodes (8px dot in `#4a4741`). Each stop contains arrival estimates in `caption`, location title in `Work Sans` Semibold, and cultural highlights in `body-sm`.
- **Dividers**: Clean 1px `#e1dcd3` horizontal lines with no drop shadow.

### Cultural Guide Specialized Components
- **Audio Chronometer Bar**: Pinned pill player with floating elevation (`box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(28, 27, 26, 0.08)`), featuring monument voice guide waveform, narration speed toggles, and dual-language selector (Hindi / English).
- **Safety Advisory Ribbon**: Top canvas bar in deep ink band (`#19191c`) with crisp `#ffffff` text and an amber `#ddaa61` alert indicator for immediate weather warnings or local festival traffic advisories.
