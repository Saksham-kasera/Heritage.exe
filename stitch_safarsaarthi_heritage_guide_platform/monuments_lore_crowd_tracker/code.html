The Optical Illusion of the Main Gateway

As you approach the Darwaza-i-Rauza (Great Gate), the Taj Mahal appears imposing and intimately near. Stepping backwards causes the mausoleum to seemingly expand rather than shrink, designed by Ustad Ahmad Lahori to evoke profound awe upon entering paradise gardens.
